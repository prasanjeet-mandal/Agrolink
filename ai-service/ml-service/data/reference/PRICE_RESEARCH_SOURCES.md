# Price research used to calibrate development anchors

- Government OGD/Agmarknet daily mandi price resource: https://data.gov.in/resource/current-daily-price-various-commodities-various-markets-mandi
- Roorkee APMC Agmarknet-linked prices: https://acrop.app/mandi/uttarakhand/haridwar/roorkee
- e-NAM official commodity list: https://enam.gov.in/commodity/commodity-list
- Department of Consumer Affairs national wholesale/retail price cross-check: https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/Default.aspx

The generated records are SYNTHETIC DEVELOPMENT DATA calibrated to researched price levels.
They are not historical Roorkee transactions. Production should replace/augment them with
verified Agmarknet/e-NAM/local records.
